# Keenwiix Store

English | [Русский](README.ru.md)

**A fashion store that looks like your brand and takes orders where your customers already talk to you.**

Live demo: https://keenwiix.github.io/keenwiix-store/

## The problem

Small fashion brands and independent designers have two bad options.

- **Big store platforms** are heavy, built from the same templates, and make every brand look the same. Setting one up takes weeks.
- **Selling through messages only** means screenshots, scattered photos and "how much is the coat?" in every chat. There is no catalog, no bag, and no clear way to place an order.

Customers drop off before the first purchase, and the owner spends the day answering the same questions.

## The solution

Keenwiix Store is a focused, good-looking storefront. It gives the brand a real catalog and a shopping bag, and sends the order straight to Telegram, the place where the brand already talks to its customers.

## What your customers get

- **A brand, not a template.** Dark editorial design, large photography and quiet typography that put the clothes first.
- **A store that works on every screen.** Phone, tablet and desktop, with a mobile menu and a bag that is always one tap away.
- **A bag that remembers.** Items stay in the bag after a page reload or a return visit. Quantities, totals and a free shipping hint update instantly.
- **A simple way to order.** One button opens the chat with the store, and the order list is already copied, ready to paste.
- **Answers where they are.** The contact page leads straight to Telegram.
- **Fast pages.** A lightweight site without heavy frameworks. Images load as the customer scrolls.

## What you get as the owner

- **Orders arrive in Telegram.** No new dashboard to learn and no orders to miss.
- **Your own site.** No platform lock-in. The site can be hosted for free, for example on GitHub Pages.
- **Easy to update.** A product is one line in a list and one card on the page.
- **Room to grow.** A clear, small codebase that is simple to extend with new pages and features.
